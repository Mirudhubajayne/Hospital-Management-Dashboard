#  Hospital Management Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Hospital Management Dashboard** developed using **Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**.

The dashboard analyzes patient demographics, hospital departments, medical conditions, treatment costs, and patient satisfaction to provide meaningful insights into hospital operations.

##  Objectives

* Analyze the overall patient distribution
* Understand gender and age demographics
* Identify the most common medical conditions
* Analyze patient distribution across departments
* Compare treatment costs between departments
* Understand patient satisfaction levels
* Calculate key hospital performance metrics

##  Key Performance Indicators

| KPI                    |    Value |
| ---------------------- | -------: |
| Total Patients         |    1,500 |
| Average Treatment Cost | ₹127,339 |
| Recovery Rate          |    32.3% |

### 1. Gender Distribution

The dashboard compares the number of male and female patients to understand the gender composition of the hospital's patient population.

### 2. Department Distribution

Patient distribution is analyzed across major departments:

* Pediatrics
* Oncology
* General Medicine
* Cardiology
* Neurology
* Orthopedics

Pediatrics has the highest patient share at approximately **18.3%**.

### 3. Top 5 Medical Conditions

The dashboard identifies the most frequently recorded medical conditions:

* Diabetes
* Stroke
* Asthma
* Hypertension
* Fracture

Diabetes and Stroke are among the most frequently observed conditions.

### 4. Age Distribution

An age distribution analysis is performed using a histogram with a density curve to understand the spread of patients across different age groups.

### 5. Treatment Cost by Department

A box plot is used to compare treatment costs across departments and identify:

* Median treatment cost
* Cost variation
* Minimum and maximum values
* Potential outliers

### 6. Patient Satisfaction

Patient satisfaction scores from **1 to 5** are analyzed to understand the overall patient experience.

A satisfaction score of **3** has the highest number of patients in the dataset.

##  Key Insights

* The dataset contains **1,500 patients**.
* Patient distribution between males and females is relatively balanced.
* **Pediatrics** has the highest department share.
* **Diabetes** is the most frequently occurring condition among the top 5 conditions.
* Treatment costs show noticeable variation across departments.
* Patient satisfaction is concentrated around the middle satisfaction levels.
* The overall recovery rate is **32.3%**.
